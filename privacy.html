Privacy policy — Training-coach

Training-coach is a private tool used only by its owner.

  • It accesses Google Drive with the drive.file scope, which covers only the files the tool itself creates. It cannot see any other files.
  • The files it stores are the owner's own encrypted backups and the owner's own activity map, kept in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • No data is collected from other people, and nothing is shared with, sold to or sent to any third party.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
